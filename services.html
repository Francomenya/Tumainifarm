Our services

More than produce. We supply, train and advise, so you get the most from the land and the market.

Fresh produce delivery

Order what you need and we bring it to your door.

  • Weekly or one-off orders for homes and offices
  • Produce packed in clean, ventilated crates
  • Delivery times confirmed when you order

Wholesale and bulk supply

Steady supply for shops, hotels, schools and restaurants.

  • Regular volumes agreed in advance
  • Grading and sorting to your quality standard
  • Clear pricing for repeat orders

Farm visits and training days

Come and see where your food is grown, or bring your group to learn.

  • Guided walks through our fields and orchards
  • Hands-on sessions for schools and farmer groups
  • Fresh produce tasting and a chance to buy on the day

Agronomy advice and field support

Practical advice from experienced agronomists to improve your yields.

  • Crop planning, planting and spacing guidance
  • Pest, disease and nutrition management
  • Field visits and follow-up support through the season

Tell us what you would like to order or learn, and we will reply the same day.
