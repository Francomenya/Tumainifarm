About our products

Keep tomatoes at room temperature until ripe, then refrigerate. Store kale wrapped in a damp cloth in the fridge and use within a few days. Keep onions in a cool, dry, airy place away from potatoes.

Avocados and mangoes ripen after harvest. Leave them at room temperature and press gently; they are ready when they give slightly. Passion fruit is sweetest when the skin is wrinkled.

Store maize, beans and green grams in sealed containers in a cool, dry place. Soak beans overnight to shorten cooking time. Green grams cook faster and do not need long soaking.

Keep milk chilled and boil it before drinking unless it is pasteurised. Refrigerate eggs for longer shelf life. Honey never needs refrigeration, and natural crystallising is normal; warm the jar gently to make it runny again.
