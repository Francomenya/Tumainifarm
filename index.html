A farm built on hope

Tumaini Farm grows vegetables, fruit, grains and dairy for families, shops and restaurants across Kenya.

We farm with care for the soil, because healthy soil gives better food year after year. Every crop is grown with sound agronomy, harvested at the right time and delivered while it is still fresh.

Why customers choose Tumaini

  • Picked fresh. We harvest to order, so produce reaches you at its best.
